## 2. Summary

The app is a practice aid for aviation radio (quiz, spelling alphabet,
vocabulary, radio navigation, radiotelephony simulation). It works without an
account, without registration and without a server of our own. Your learning
progress (which questions you answered correctly or wrongly, the topics you
selected, a training session you have started, your display setting, your daily
goal and the number of questions answered today) is stored only in the app's
private storage on your device. It is not transmitted to the developer or to
third parties.

The only third party involved is Google Play: the optional subscription
"Zeitpass" (learning statistics) is purchased and managed through Google Play
Billing (see Section 3.2). No advertising, analytics or tracking services are
used. The app does not contain a data-collecting SDK of any kind.

The app has Android's Auto Backup switched off. Your learning progress is
therefore not copied into a backup of your Google Account by the app.

## 3. What Data Is Processed?

### 3.1 Learning Progress and Settings (local only)

The app stores on your device: for each question a small counter of how
securely you know it, the exam areas you selected, an unfinished training
session (so that you can continue it), the chosen display theme, the daily goal
and the number of questions answered on the current day, and whether you have
read the first-start notice. Practice exams are not saved.

This data never leaves your device through the app. You can erase it by
clearing the app's data in the Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

### 3.2 Google Play Billing

The optional subscription "Zeitpass" is purchased through Google Play
Billing. Payment details (card numbers, billing address and so on) are
processed entirely by Google, which acts as an independent controller for that
processing. We never see or receive them. A free trial period offered with the
subscription is also administered by Google; whether you are eligible is
decided by Google.

To find out whether the statistics are unlocked, the app queries your purchase
status from Google Play when the statistics screen is opened. The app keeps the
last result (active or not) in its local storage so that the statistics remain
available without a connection. The developer receives no purchase data from
the app. See [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 4. Permissions and Network Access

The app does not ask you for any permission. Its manifest contains the
permissions that the Google Play Billing library adds: the billing permission
(`com.android.vending.BILLING`), `android.permission.INTERNET` and
`android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`. They are standard permissions that
are granted at installation and that Google Play Billing needs to reach
Google Play. The app itself does not open any other network connection and does
not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files.

## 5. Third-Party Services

Google Play Billing (see 3.2). Nothing else. No advertising, tracking or
analytics SDKs are used (for example no Google Analytics or Firebase Analytics).
The app contains no web view and no social-media integration.

## 6. Storage and Disclosure

All data described in Section 3.1 stays in the app's private storage until you
delete it or the app. The developer does not operate a server for the app and
does not receive or disclose this data.

## 7. Rights of Data Subjects

Under the GDPR you generally have the right to access (Art. 15), rectification
(Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17) and restriction of processing (Art. 18). Where the
legal requirements are met you also have the right to data portability
(Art. 20) and the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests
(Art. 21). Because the developer receives none of the data stored in the app,
you can already exercise most of these rights yourself by clearing the app's
data. You may also contact the controller using the details in Section 1.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection
supervisory authority.

## 8. Legal Basis

Local processing of the data in Section 3.1, to provide the learning functions
you use, is based on Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of the contract under which
you use the app) or, to the extent that does not apply, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (our
legitimate interest in providing the functions you have chosen). Processing by
Google in connection with Google Play Billing is based on Google's own legal
bases as an independent controller.
